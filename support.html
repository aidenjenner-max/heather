Contact us

Questions, ideas or a problem? Email aiden@jennermediagroup.com. We read every message and reply within two working days.

Playing

  • Each day Bean picks a short plan of puzzles. There's no timer and nothing to lose.
  • Every game has a Help button with how to play, and most have a Hint button.
  • Make the words bigger in Settings → Text size.

Family circles

  • Family circles are private: only people with your family's invite link or code can join. Everyone agrees to the family circle rules first.
  • Rude words are blocked automatically.
  • Report a question: open the Family tab, tap ⋯ on the memory, then Report or hide. You can also tap Report or hide this question after answering it in Memory Lane. It's hidden for you straight away, and if two people report it, it's hidden for everyone. We review reports within 24 hours and remove anything abusive, along with the person who posted it.
  • Hide someone: tap ⋯ on one of their memories, then Report or hide → Hide everything from them. To see them again, open Manage circle and tap Show again.
  • The person who started the circle can remove questions and people.
  • Year in review: on the Family tab, look back at the year's memories and print them or save them as a keepsake.
  • Leave and delete: Family tab → Manage circle → Leave the circle → Leave and delete my questions.
