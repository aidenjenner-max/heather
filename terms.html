Family circle rules

Family circles are private groups for family and close friends. When you start or join one, you agree to these rules. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people.

  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment, threats or hateful remarks about anyone.
  • No sexual, violent, crude or otherwise objectionable content.
  • Don't share someone else's private details (addresses, phone numbers, health or money matters) without their permission.
  • Only invite people you know.

What we do about problems

  • Rude words are blocked automatically before a memory or name is saved.
  • Anyone can report a memory (it disappears for them straight away; after two reports it's hidden for everyone) and hide everything from a person.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and remove the person who posted it from the circle.

Your memories

What you add stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people in your circle so the circle works. You can delete everything you added at any time: Family tab → Manage circle → Leave the circle → Leave and delete my questions.
