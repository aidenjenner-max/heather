Contact us

Questions, ideas or a problem? Email aiden@jennermediagroup.com. We read every message and reply within two working days.

Playing

  • Each day Bean picks a short plan of puzzles. There's no timer and nothing to lose.
  • Every game has a Help button with how to play, and most have a Hint button.
  • Make the words bigger in Settings → Text size.

Family circles

  • Family circles are private: only people with your family's invite link can join.
  • Rude words are blocked automatically.
  • Report a question: tap Report next to it in Settings, or Report or hide this question after answering it in Memory Lane. It's hidden for you straight away, and if two people report it, it's hidden for everyone. We review reports and remove anything abusive.
  • Hide someone: in Settings → Family circle, tap Hide next to their name to stop seeing their questions.
  • The person who started the circle can remove questions and people.
  • Leave and delete: Settings → Family circle → Leave the circle → Leave and delete my questions.
