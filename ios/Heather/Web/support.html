Playing

  • Each day Bean picks a short plan of puzzles. There's no timer and nothing to lose.
  • Every game has a Help button with how to play, and most have a Hint button.
  • Make the words bigger in Settings → Text size.

Family circles

  • Family circles are private: only people with your family's invite link can join.
  • Rude words are blocked automatically.
  • Report a question: open the family circle (from Home or Settings), tap ⋯ on the memory, then Report or hide. You can also tap Report or hide this question after answering it in Memory Lane. It's hidden for you straight away, and if two people report it, it's hidden for everyone. We review reports and remove anything abusive.
  • Hide someone: on the family circle page, open Manage circle and tap Hide next to their name.
  • The person who started the circle can remove questions and people.
  • Leave and delete: family circle page → Manage circle → Leave the circle → Leave and delete my questions.
