Privacy

Heather · daily brain games · updated 24 November 2026

Heather is a free set of gentle brain games. We keep what we collect to the minimum the app needs, and we never sell it.

If you play without signing up

Your garden (games played, scores, settings and your first name) is saved only in your web browser on your own device. We don't receive it.

If you sign in with Facebook

  • Facebook shares your name and email address with us. We don't ask for your friends, photos or posts, and we never post on your behalf.
  • We save your garden to your account so you can pick up on another phone or tablet.
  • We may note who invited you, so we can see which invitations help people find Heather.

Family circles

If you join a family circle, the name you type and the memory questions you add are shared with the other people in that circle. Anyone can leave a circle at any time in Settings.

Where it's kept

Accounts and family circles are stored with Supabase, and the website is hosted by Vercel. Both act only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

Open Heather, go to Settings → Your account → Delete my account. This deletes your account, the garden saved to it, and removes you from your family circle straight away.

You can also remove Heather from Facebook under Facebook Settings → Apps and websites. To clear a garden kept only on your device, use Settings → Start fresh or clear your browser's data.
