Privacy

Heather · daily brain games · updated 26 November 2026

Heather is a set of gentle brain games, on the website and in the iPhone and iPad app. We collect as little as possible, we never sell it, and there are no ads or trackers.

Your garden

Your garden (games played, scores, settings and your first name) is saved only on your own device: in your web browser, or inside the Heather app. We don't receive it.

Daily reminder (app only)

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone or tablet shows it at the time you pick. It's set up on your device; nothing is sent to us. The app's icon can also show how many new family memories are waiting.

Sharing

When you invite someone or share Heather, your phone's own sharing options or Facebook open with a message and a link. You decide what to send. We don't receive your contacts or anything from Facebook.

Family circles

If you start or join a family circle, we store the first name you type, the memory questions you add, and whether each person got each memory right, so the circle works. Only people in your circle can see them. Reports are kept so we can review them. The Year in Review is made on your device from your circle's memories; printing it happens on your device.

Family pass (website)

If someone shares a family pass with you, we store the first name you type and a random code for your device, so the pass knows who is on it. Payments on the website, when offered, are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

Children

Heather is made for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Where it's kept

Family circles and family passes are stored with Supabase in Canada, and the website is hosted by Vercel. Both act only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

To leave a family circle and delete everything you added: Family tab → Manage circle → Leave the circle → Leave and delete my questions. To clear your garden, use Settings → Start fresh, delete the app, or clear your browser's data. To ask us to delete anything else, email us.
