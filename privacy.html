Privacy

Heather · daily brain games · updated 24 November 2026

Heather is a free set of gentle brain games. We keep what we collect to the minimum the app needs, and we never sell it.

Your garden

Your garden (games played, scores, settings and your first name) is saved only in your web browser on your own device. We don't receive it.

Sharing on Facebook

If you choose to share Heather on Facebook, Facebook opens with a link to Heather. You decide what to post. We don't receive anything from Facebook.

Family circles

If you join a family circle, the name you type and the memory questions you add are shared with the other people in that circle. Anyone can leave a circle at any time in Settings.

Where it's kept

Family circles are stored with Supabase, and the website is hosted by Vercel. Both act only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

To leave a family circle, open Heather and go to Settings → Family circle → Leave the circle. To clear your garden, use Settings → Start fresh or clear your browser's data.
